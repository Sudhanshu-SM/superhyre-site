SUPERHYRE SIGN-UP STORYBOARD: ALL PROMPTS (14 images)
==================================================================

THE STORY: "Your first morning with SuperHyre"
The same warm desk from the sign-in artwork, seen from above, in every frame: the plant
top-left, the white coffee mug, the orange notebook, the charcoal tablet or laptop. Only
what is on the screen and the desk changes, so the frames read as one continuous morning.
Each frame fills the left panel of the sign-in frame during its step of sign-up, with a
short caption card under it.

  Frame  Step               What is happening                                         Caption under it
  F1     I. Hello           They've just signed in. SuperHyre says hello and asks their name. "Every search starts with a hello."
  F2     II. Your company   SuperHyre reads their company's public website.           "We read your public site, nothing else."
  F3     II. Your company   What we learned: three facts they can check and fix.      "Facts you can fix, each with its source."
  F4     II. Your company   Who they hire for, and their part in hiring.              "Who you hire for, and your part in it."
  F5     III. First role    The first role they want to fill.                         "One role to start with."
  F6     IV. Your look      Their job post, in their own brand colours and type.      "Your job posts and forms, in your colours."
  F7     V. First steps     They pick how to start; the work is sent off to the agent. "Pick one and the agent gets going."
  F8     Welcome            Set-up done: the dashboard comes alive with the first candidates. "You're set up."
  F9     Side path          Their team is already on SuperHyre: ask to join.          "Ask to join; an admin lets you in."
  F10    Side path          Waiting for an admin to approve them.                     "Your personal workspace is ready meanwhile."
  F11    Side path          We couldn't read their website: a small hiccup.           "Try another link, or tell us yourself."
  F12    Side path          Their workspace is being set up (a few seconds).          "A few seconds."
  F13    Side path          They signed in with a personal email: use your work email. "Use your work email."
  F14    Side path          Their invite link has expired.                            "Ask for a new one."

Order: F1 to F8 is the main path, in sequence. F9 to F14 are side paths.

GENERATE THEM ONE BY ONE
- One prompt = one image. Paste ONE prompt, generate, save it, then the next.
- No grids, sheets, collages or side-by-side variations in one image.
- Save each with the exact file name shown (for example F1-hello.png).
- All frames are portrait 2:3, 1024x1536, like the sign-in art.
- Attach superhyre-site/assets/access-art.webp as the style reference if your tool allows,
  and keep the SAME desk, plant, mug and notebook look from frame to frame.
- Keep the top third and the bottom sixth of each frame calm: the wordmark sits at the
  top and the caption card at the bottom.
