## Bugs found and fixed during the run
1. The chat's keyword fast path swallowed requests: "add a note in Notion saying outreach starts Monday" returned the outreach view; "which of my campaigns need attention" returned the plain campaign list; "schedule an email…" would have opened the interview-scheduling view. Fixed in `packages/shared/src/views.ts` (outward or time-bound requests go to the model) and `agent/chat.ts` (the campaign-list shortcut is now a whitelist of exact phrasings). Tests added.
2. Console CSS: a bad splice in the sign-up scene stylesheet had deleted the hero and leaf sizing (the screenshot you sent); restored.
3. Test-data hygiene: the load test created 6,600 tasks in the fn3 workspace; they and the other E2E rows were deleted.

## Known limits and things to look at
- Spec drafting from a JD took ~100 s and batch judging often hits the gateway's 120 s limit when the gateway is busy: the model gateway on Haiku is the slow part, not the API.
- Replies cannot be read (Gmail is send-only), so "follow up only if no reply" relies on the recruiter logging replies.
- `db/live/09_superhyre_platform.sql` is generated and still stops at migration 170; 171 (integrations), 172 (memory activity) and 173 (scheduled actions) must be added (rebuild with `server/scripts/live-sql.ts`) before the first live run. Nothing is deployed.
- Slack/Notion/Google were exercised against simulators; real tokens/consent have not been tried.
- The memory page lists many "what happened" notes per campaign (episodes); fine for now, could be folded.
