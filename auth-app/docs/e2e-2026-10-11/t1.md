## S1. Type, centring, contrast, pills, scroll: PASS (S3 notes)

Measured on the real set-up screens (seven beats) at 1440x900, 1280x720, 1100x800, 820x1000, 390x844, light and with
`prefers-color-scheme: dark`. Contrast method: render the page with all text transparent, sample the real background
under every text run, composite the computed text colour (with opacity) over the worst pixel. Thresholds: 4.5 normal,
3 large (24 px, or 18.7 px bold). Also repeated in the live flow (sign-up typed through, resized at every beat, long
role title, 3x JD, 70-character client name): no horizontal or vertical scroll at any size, any beat.
