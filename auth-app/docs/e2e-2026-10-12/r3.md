## Environment notes that shaped the run

1. The gateway is shared by every tester. In the last 400 gateway log lines at 19:20 about 45 percent of `POST /v1/superhyre/runs`
   (spec, sourcing, judging, memory) were refused with 503 "at capacity" ("every slot of a lane is busy"). Chat turns were mostly
   served. Spec drafting from a pasted JD failed three times out of four attempts (F1) and succeeded once, after 57 s.
2. `own@fn3qa.dev` reached the per-person daily chat quota ("You've sent your 200 agent messages for today", resets 05:30 IST). The
   quota is counted for every tester who signs in as the demo owner. The rest of the demo work ran as `adm@fn3qa.dev` and
   `meera@fn3qa.dev`.
3. Every latency below was measured under that shared load. The page's first-words time equals the server's `firstTokenMs`
   within 0.3 s, so the wait is the model's, not the console's.
4. Harness note: SSE timestamps include a 0.4 s connect pause; the tables below have it subtracted.
