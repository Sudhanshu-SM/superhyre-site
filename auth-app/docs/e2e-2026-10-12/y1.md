## D3: "create a new campaign called Data Analyst for the client Orchid Bank in Pune", Orchid Bank not in Clients

**FAIL S2: the offer is made, but choosing it is lost: both the card and the result say "Internal hire", and neither the client nor

"Internal hire" and `create()` never calls `saveClient`. The unit tests in `fa2Agent.test.ts` cover the question and the draft,
not the card after the answer.

S3: a campaign with the same name as an open one is allowed with the warning "You already have an open campaign called Data
Analyst." (correct); the view's `campaignName` for the new-campaign form is the existing campaign of that name.
