SUPERHYRE ART v3: EVERY PROMPT, READY TO PASTE
===============================================

How to use
----------
- Attach the sign-in artwork (superhyre-site/assets/access-art.webp) as the style
  reference if your generator accepts one. Everything matches that look.
- Paste one prompt at a time; each is complete. Ask for 3 or 4 variations and keep the one
  closest to the sign-in art, with no garbled text (screens and cards show only bars).
- Save each pick as "<ID> <name>.png" (for example "E3 empty-columns.png") in one folder
  and tell me where it is. I make the web sizes and put each one in its place.

Why the families look different
-------------------------------
One world (the warm desk from the sign-in art), framed differently per family so nothing
repeats: the sign-up story is the top-down desk; empty states are close-up still lifes of
one object; Home workflows show the result you'll get as a floating card; success moments
are caught mid-motion at a low angle; working states are layered so they can move; system
states are minimal and quiet; candidate pages show the applicant's own desk at dusk; email
and link previews are landscape with room for a title. Every object appears once.
