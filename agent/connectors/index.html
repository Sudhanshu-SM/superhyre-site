Meetings

Meeting assistant …
An extension for Chrome. Leave a Google Meet and the summary is on your WhatsApp: what was decided, who promised what, by when. Every call also becomes a row in a sheet in your own Drive. The audio is never saved.
Open

It records a call only after you click, once per call, and Chrome shows the tab is being shared the whole time.

Memory

What your assistant knows
Every person, company and file it has worked out from your tools, how it would describe you, and where each piece came from. Delete anything that's wrong or that you'd rather it didn't know.
Open

It keeps the catalogue and the conclusions, never copies of your email, your documents or your messages. Nothing there is shared with your teammates.
