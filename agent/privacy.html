Privacy Policy

SuperHyre WhatsApp assistant · Effective date: 2 October 2026

SuperHyre provides a hiring-coordination assistant on WhatsApp that helps recruiters, candidates and hiring managers share availability, schedule interviews and receive reminders. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, mobile number, time zone, and your role (recruiter, candidate or hiring manager).
  • Messages: the text messages, voice notes, photos, files, contact cards and locations you send to the SuperHyre WhatsApp number, and the replies we send you.
  • Memory: so the assistant remembers what you have told it (for example the people you work with, what was agreed and your preferences), we keep your chat history and turn it into notes the assistant can look up later. The notes are written with the help of our AI language model provider and stored in our own database. Drafts waiting for your approval and one-time sign-in links are not added to them.
  • Scheduling data: availability you share, interviews and meetings arranged through SuperHyre, and reminders.
  • Connections: which recruiter invited you and in what role.
  • Technical data: message delivery status and basic logs needed to run and secure the service.
  • Connected tools (recruiters, optional): if you connect Google Workspace, Notion or Slack, see the sections below.

Google Workspace (optional, recruiters only)

Recruiters can choose to connect their Google account so the assistant can work with their Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Sheets. Connecting is never required to use SuperHyre.

  • What we access: your Google account email address; your Gmail messages, to search, read and summarise them, to archive, star or mark them read when you ask, and to send emails you approve (the assistant cannot permanently delete email); your Google Calendar events, to show your schedule and create or cancel events you ask for, including interviews booked through SuperHyre; your Google Drive files, to find and read the files you ask about; your Google Docs, to create documents or add to them when you ask; and your Google Sheets, to read them and to update or add rows you ask for.
  • When we access it: only when you ask the assistant to do something with it, and when an interview is booked through SuperHyre (to add it to your calendar). We do not scan your mailbox or files in the background.
  • What we store: an encrypted Google access token, your Google email address, the permissions you granted, emails the assistant writes for you while they wait for your approval (the draft is deleted once the email is sent or discarded; we keep the recipients, subject, time and Gmail message ID, so the assistant can follow up in the same thread, and the preview it showed you stays in your chat history), plus the event ID and Meet link of interviews added to your calendar. Your existing email, calendar, file and sheet contents are not stored by us; they are fetched when needed to answer you and passed to our AI language model provider only to carry out your request. The assistant's replies to you on WhatsApp, which may summarise them, are kept as part of your chat history and memory.
  • Sending on your behalf: the assistant shows you each email it writes, exactly as it will be sent, and sends it only after you confirm in a later message.
  • Disconnecting: you can disconnect at any time from your SuperHyre page or by telling the assistant "disconnect Google". We then delete the stored token, so we can no longer access your account. You can also remove access from your Google Account settings.

SuperHyre's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, do not use it to train AI models, and do not let people read it except with your permission, for security purposes, or where required by law.

Notion (optional, recruiters only)

Recruiters can choose to connect Notion so the assistant can work with their Notion pages and databases. Connecting is never required to use SuperHyre.

  • What we access: only the pages and databases you select on Notion's consent screen (or share with the connection later), and your Notion name and email address. The assistant can search them by title, read them and their comments, add rows to databases and update their fields, create pages, add text to pages and add comments, when you ask.
  • When we access it: only when you ask the assistant to do something with it. We do not read your Notion in the background.
  • What we store: an encrypted Notion access token, your workspace name and your email address. Page and database contents are not stored by us; they are fetched when needed to answer you and passed to our AI language model provider only to carry out your request.
  • Disconnecting: from your SuperHyre page or by telling the assistant "disconnect Notion". We then delete the stored token. You can also remove the connection in Notion under Settings, Connections.

Slack (optional, recruiters only)

Recruiters can choose to connect Slack so the assistant can search, read and post in their Slack workspace as them. Connecting is never required to use SuperHyre.

  • What we access: your workspace's list of channels and people; and, as you and only where you yourself have access, message search, the messages of channels and threads you ask about, posting messages you approve, and adding emoji reactions you ask for. We do not read direct messages.
  • When we access it: only when you ask the assistant to do something with it. We do not read your Slack in the background.
  • What we store: encrypted Slack access tokens, your workspace name and your email address, and Slack messages the assistant writes for you while they wait for your approval (the content is deleted once posted or discarded; we keep only the channel and time). Existing Slack messages are not stored by us; they are fetched when needed to answer you and passed to our AI language model provider only to carry out your request.
  • Posting on your behalf: the assistant shows you each message exactly as it will be posted, and posts it under your name only after you confirm in a later message.
  • Disconnecting: from your SuperHyre page or by telling the assistant "disconnect Slack". We then delete the stored tokens, so we can no longer access your Slack.

How we use your information

  • To verify your number and set up your account.
  • To understand your requests and carry them out, such as collecting availability, booking interviews and sending reminders.
  • To pass scheduling details and messages between you and the people you are coordinating with, only as needed for that purpose.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent misuse and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • People in your hiring process: for example, a candidate's availability is shared with their recruiter and assigned hiring manager. We do not share one candidate's details with other candidates.
  • Service providers that help us run SuperHyre, under agreements that limit their use of your data: WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) to deliver messages, cloud hosting and database providers, an AI language model provider to interpret your messages and turn your chat into notes, and a speech-to-text provider to transcribe voice notes.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the service. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time, and we will do so unless we are required by law to keep it.

Your choices and rights

  • You can stop using SuperHyre at any time by no longer messaging the SuperHyre number, or by blocking it in WhatsApp.
  • Depending on where you live, laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or the GDPR may give you rights to access, correct or delete your information. Email us at the address below to use them.

Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and other reasonable measures to protect your information. No system is completely secure, so please do not share sensitive information that is not needed for scheduling.

Children

SuperHyre is a business tool intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know through the service.

Contact

Questions or data requests: team@superhyre.com (SuperHyre, Bangalore, India).
