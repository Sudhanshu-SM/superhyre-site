What it does

  • Send a JD as a PDF, a Word file or just text. It reads it, including scans.
  • Say how many people you want. Twenty if you don't say.
  • It searches the web across several angles: the exact title, the titles people give themselves, the industries they come from, the companies worth asking.
  • You get a sheet with each person's current role, company, experience, location, why they fit, what to watch out for, and their LinkedIn.
  • Ask again for the same role and you get a new worksheet in the same sheet, with people you haven't seen before.
  • Your JDs are kept as documents in your own Drive, so you can open them later and never have to send one twice.

It needs Google connected, because the JD and the sheet live in your Drive, not ours. Candidates are found through a search index, so the sheet asks you to check the live profile before you reach out. A JD is yours: nothing in it is ever sent to a candidate or a hiring manager unless you tell your assistant to pass something on.

Switching on or off happens here. Your assistant will say so on WhatsApp either way.
