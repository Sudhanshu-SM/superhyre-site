Personal assistant …

The one that comes with your account. Connect your tools and it works in them for you.

Connect Google first, then come back and switch this on. Connect Google

What it does

  • Email: find it, read it, write it. It shows you anything it has written and sends it only once you say so.
  • Calendar: book, move and cancel, with a Meet link and invitations to the right people.
  • Drive, Docs and Sheets: read a file, write a document, add rows to a sheet.
  • Notion and Slack: find things in the pages you shared, add rows, summarise a channel, post as you after you approve it.
  • It watches for the replies you are waiting on and tells you when they arrive, so you never have to check.
  • Reminders without being asked, standing rules said once, and it remembers what you tell it.

This one can't be switched off: it is your assistant. What it can reach is up to you, on the connectors page.

Switching on or off happens here. Your assistant will say so on WhatsApp either way.
