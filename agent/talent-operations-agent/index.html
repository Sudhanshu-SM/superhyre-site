Talent operations agent …

Jobs, candidates, hiring managers and interviews, run from WhatsApp.

Connect Google first, then come back and switch this on. Connect Google

What it does

  • Tell it a company and a role and it opens a job with two invite links: one for candidates, one for the hiring manager.
  • Whoever uses a link joins in that role. No role picker, nothing to explain.
  • It asks your candidates when they are free, collects the answers and shares them with the hiring manager.
  • It books the interview, puts it on your calendar with a Meet link, and reminds everyone before it.
  • Ask it anything about where things stand: who has replied, what is scheduled, what is waiting on you.
  • Bring your recruiting team in with one invite link, and ask what any of them is working on.

Candidates and hiring managers get their own assistant on WhatsApp when they join, and they only ever see their own part of your hiring.

Switching on or off happens here. Your assistant will say so on WhatsApp either way.
