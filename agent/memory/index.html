What I keep, and what I don't

I keep a catalogue and the conclusions I drew from it: who you deal with, what your files are called and where they live, how you work. I never keep copies of your email, your documents or your messages. Those stay in Gmail, Drive, Notion and Slack, which are the real thing and have better search than I could build; when I need what one of them actually says, I open it there and then.

So what is below is the whole of it. If something here is wrong, delete it — it goes from the next message I write, and the file, email or message it was drawn from is untouched.

None of this is shared with anyone: not your teammates, not whoever owns your team. Everyone's assistant reads only their own tools. You can also correct me in chat — "forget that Acme is a client" — and I'll keep that as a standing correction, which is the one thing a delete here doesn't do.
