The best startup hires don't come from job boards, they come from understanding exactly where a company is in its arc and what kind of person thrives at that inflection point.

At SuperHyre, we've built our entire process around that insight. We call it talent engineering - applying an engineering and first-principles mindset to hunting elite talent, rather than doing traditional HR work.

That means we don't start with a database and a keyword search. We start with the problem: what does this role need to solve, what does the ideal person look like from first principles, and where are they most likely hiding.

Our sourcing is AI-native, our shortlists are ruthlessly filtered, and our judgment is calibrated against hundreds of startup hiring patterns.

We don't hand you a stack of profiles to sift through.

We hand you a decision.
